private chat room

Direct WebRTC connection using public STUN for NAT traversal — no chat server, no relay, no TURN. Signaling (codes) is exchanged manually between participants. Because TURN is intentionally not used, a direct connection isn't guaranteed for every network: some NATs, firewalls, corporate networks, and mobile networks may prevent it, especially between two participants who are both behind restrictive networks.

NETWORK (STUN)

Google's public STUN servers are used by default so this can work over the open internet, not just a LAN. STUN only helps two sides discover a reachable address — it can't force a connection through every network. If you or the other participant are behind a restrictive NAT, firewall, corporate network, or mobile carrier network, and no direct path can be found, that connection will fail (there is no TURN relay fallback, by design). If that happens, try your own STUN server(s) below (comma-separated) — they'll be tried alongside Google's — or a less restrictive network.
